    How the score works
    • Four signals, each scored 0 to 100: momentum (median engagement on the latest 12 posts, posting cadence, audience size), retail (verified GCC retailers and stockists, bonus for more than one country), HBI fit (outside Huda Beauty's makeup and fragrance core, female founder, GCC-born, no overlap with current holdings), and still unpriced (no disclosed round scores highest, a Series A lowest).
    • Invest weights them 30 / 30 / 25 / 15. Acquire weights retail 45, audience size 25, fit 30. Incubate weights momentum 30, fit 35, unpriced 35, and ignores retail.
    • Weekly refresh: followers and post counts are re-scraped every Monday. Growth is shown against the 16 November baseline. Engagement stays at the baseline reading.
    • Limits: paid ads can lift likes. "Not disclosed" does not mean unfunded. Retail lists come from public sources and may be incomplete.
